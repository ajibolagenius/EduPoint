# Assignment 01 — Tutor Notes: The CampusChop Case File

**100L · Semester 1 · Covers weeks 1–3 · For tutors only**

These notes go with the student brief, [Assignment 01 — The CampusChop Case File](assignment-01.md). Read
the brief first: the tasks, rules, submission steps and marking scheme are all there.

## What this assignment is for

It is a take-home consolidation of weeks 1–3, set at the end of week 3. Every task needs ideas from at
least two weeks, which shows you whether students can **connect** the weeks, not just recall each one.
It needs no internet, and students may work on paper or on a laptop, so every student can complete it.

- **Deadline and weight.** The curriculum document doesn't schedule this assignment. Set the deadline
  yourself, and allow at least a week. It doesn't change the programme's grading weights; confirm with
  the tutor team where its mark is recorded.
- **Printing.** Give every student a printed copy. Students without a device can't open the linked
  notes, so make sure they have their printed notes from weeks 1–3.

## Answer key

This assignment is **graded**, so its answer key is **not** in this public repository. Get it from the
tutor team's private channel. Don't post answers in the class group, even after the deadline: future
classes may get the same case file.

## Checking that the work is the student's own

- Pick three or four students at random in week 5. Ask each to explain one Task 2 ticket and one Task 4
  sentence aloud, without their script.
- Neither handwriting nor typing proves the work is unaided: a student can copy AI output by hand, and
  typed work is easier to paste into. The live questions are the check, so treat paper and laptop
  submissions the same. Don't accuse anyone because of writing style alone. Follow your institution's
  process.

## What to look for

These are prompts for feedback, not model answers.

| What you see | What it means | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| The app drawn with its own separate backend or database | They've missed that one backend serves many frontends | "Where does the app get the menu from? Is it the same menu as the website's?" |
| ★ placed on the browser or on the database | They're unsure who *decides* | "Who can read code that runs in the browser? Does the database decide, or remember?" |
| HTTPS drawn as a box | HTTPS is still a *thing* to them, not a property of a link | "What did the sealed envelope in the week 1 lab protect, and on which part of the trip?" |
| Ticket 8 treated as a bug | They've forgotten the local copy on the phone | "Which apps in your airplane-mode table partly worked? Why?" |
| Task 4 sources say "Google" or "the internet" | Not yet a real check | "Which page, in which week's notes?" |
| Task 4 marks every sentence *Wrong* | Over-correcting after week 3 | "Which of these did your notes agree with?" |
| Task 5 paragraph lists the weeks one by one with no links between them | Recall without connection | "What does week 2 need from week 1? What does week 3 need from both?" |
